7). what is Gen AI?

Generative AI (Gen AI) is a type of Artificial Intelligence that creates new content such as text, images, videos, music, and computer code. It learns from large amounts of existing data to generatehuman-like responses. Gen AI helps users complete tasks faster and improve creativity. It is widely used in education, business, healthcare, and software development. Examples of Generative AI include ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot.
